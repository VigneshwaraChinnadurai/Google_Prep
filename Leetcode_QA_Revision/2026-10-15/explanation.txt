Title: Subarrays Distinct Element Sum of Squares I
URL: https://leetcode.com/problems/subarrays-distinct-element-sum-of-squares-i/

LLM Explanation:
Brute force O(n²). For each starting index i, expand right tracking distinct elements via a set. Add len(seen)² at each step. With n≤100, O(n²)=10000 ops is trivial.

LLM Testcase Validation:

Test 2: [1,1] → 1+1+1=3 ✓
