Question:
You are given an integer array nums and an integer target . Return the number of subarrays of nums in which target is the majority element . The majority element of a subarray is the element that appears strictly more than half of the times in that subarray. Example 1: Input: nums = [1,2,2,3], target = 2 Output: 5 Explanation: Valid subarrays with target = 2 as the majority element: nums[1..1] = [2] nums[2..2] = [2] nums[1..2] = [2,2] nums[0..2] = [1,2,2] nums[1..3] = [2,2,3] So there are 5 such subarrays. Example 2: Input: nums = [1,1,1,1], target = 1 Output: 10 Explanation: ​​​​​​​ All 10 subarrays have 1 as the majority element. Example 3: Input: nums = [1,2,3], target = 4 Output: 0 Explanation: target = 4 does not appear in nums at all. Therefore, there cannot be any subarray where 4 is the majority element. Hence the answer is 0. Constraints: 1 &lt;= nums.length &lt;= 1000 1 &lt;= nums[i] &lt;= 10 ​​​​​​​9 1 &lt;= target &lt;= 10 9
