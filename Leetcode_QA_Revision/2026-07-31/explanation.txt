Title: Minimum Number of Pushes to Type Word II
URL: https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-number-of-pushes-to-type-word-ii/

LLM Explanation:
The problem asks for the minimum number of pushes to type a given `word` by optimally remapping letters to 8 telephone keys (2-9). The number of pushes for a letter depends on its position on a key: the first letter costs 1 push, the second costs 2, and so on.

### Optimal Strategy: Greedy Approach

To minimize the total number of pushes, we should assign the most frequently occurring characters to the positions that require the fewest pushes. This is a classic greedy strategy. The total cost is the sum of `frequency * push_cost` for each unique character. To minimize this sum, we must pair the largest frequencies with the smallest push costs.

The available push costs are determined by the 8 keys:
- There are 8 slots available that cost 1 push each (the first position on each of the 8 keys).
- There are 8 slots that cost 2 pushes each (the second position on each key).
- There are 8 slots that cost 3 pushes each, and so on.

### Algorithm Steps

1.  **Count Frequencies**: First, we count the occurrences of each character in the `word`. A hash map (like `collections.Counter`) is ideal for this.

2.  **Sort Frequencies**: We extract the frequencies of all unique characters and sort them in descending order. This places the most common characters first.

3.  **Assign Costs and Calculate Total**: We iterate through the sorted list of frequencies. The character with the highest frequency gets the cheapest cost, the second-highest gets the next cheapest, and so on.
    - The 8 most frequent characters are assigned to the 1-push slots.
    - The next 8 most frequent (9th to 16th) are assigned to the 2-push slots.
    - The next 8 (17th to 24th) are assigned to the 3-push slots.
    - The remaining (25th and 26th) are assigned to the 4-push slots.

    A simple formula can determine the cost for the character at index `i` (0-indexed) in the sorted frequency list: `push_cost = (i // 8) + 1`. We multiply each character's frequency by its assigned push cost and sum these values to get the minimum total pushes.

### Complexity Analysis

-   **Time Complexity**: O(N), where N is the length of the `word`.
    -   Counting character frequencies takes O(N) time.
    -   The number of unique characters is at most 26. Sorting these frequencies takes O(U log U), where U <= 26, which is constant time, O(1).
    -   The final loop runs at most 26 times, also O(1).
    -   Thus, the dominant operation is the initial frequency count.

-   **Space Complexity**: O(1).
    -   The hash map for frequencies and the list of frequencies will store at most 26 entries, corresponding to the letters of the alphabet. This is constant space.

### Edge Cases

-   **Short words**: The logic holds. If there are 8 or fewer unique characters, all will be assigned a push cost of 1.
-   **Words with many unique characters**: The algorithm correctly assigns increasing costs to less frequent characters as the 1-push, 2-push, etc., slots are filled.
-   **Words with one repeated character**: The single unique character will be assigned a cost of 1, and the total pushes will be its frequency.
