Title: Minimum Changes to Make K Semi-palindromes
URL: https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-changes-to-make-k-semi-palindromes/

LLM Explanation:
Step 1: for every substring s[i..j] of length L >= 2, compute its cost as the minimum, over divisors d of L with d < L, of the changes needed to make each residue group a palindrome. For a divisor d, group r holds the characters at i+r, i+r+d, and so on, with m = L/d characters. The group needs one change for every mirrored pair that differs, comparing position t with position m-1-t for t < m//2. Substrings of length 1 have no valid divisor, so their cost is infinite.

Step 2: dp over partitions. Let dp[p][j] be the minimum cost to split the first j characters into p semi-palindromic parts. Then dp[p][j] = min over i of dp[p-1][i] + cost[i][j-1], with dp[0][0] = 0. Rows are rolled so only two arrays are kept. The answer is dp[k][n].

Time complexity: the cost table takes about the sum over all substrings of L times the number of divisors of L, which is a few million operations for n = 200. The DP takes O(k * n^2), at most about 4 million operations. Space complexity: O(n^2) for the cost table.

Edge cases:
- Single-character parts are never allowed, and the infinite cost enforces that.
- k = 1 returns cost[0][n-1].
- Substrings that are already semi-palindromes cost 0, as in "aabbaa" with k=3.
- A substring of length 2 can only use d=1, so it needs one change unless both letters are equal.

LLM Testcase Validation:
"abcac", k=2: cost("ab") = 1 (d=1, one mismatch), cost("cac") = 0 (d=1, palindrome). Split "ab" + "cac" gives 1. Other splits: "abc" + "ac" gives 1 + 1 = 2. "a" + "bcac" and "abca" + "c" are infinite, since a length-1 substring has no valid divisor. The minimum is 1. Expected 1. PASS
