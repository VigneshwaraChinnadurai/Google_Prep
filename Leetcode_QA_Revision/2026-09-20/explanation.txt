Title: Reverse Degree of a String
URL: https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-degree-of-a-string/

LLM Explanation:
Direct simulation. Reverse alphabet value of char c = 26 - (ord(c) - ord('a')). Multiply by 1-indexed position and sum. O(n) time, O(1) space.
