Title: Maximize Sum of Squares of Digits
URL: https://leetcode.com/problems/maximize-sum-of-squares-of-digits/

LLM Explanation:
APPROACH: Greedy digit assignment — O(num).

To maximise sum of squares with a fixed digit sum:
  By convexity of x², concentrating value (using larger digits) maximises sum of squares.
  Greedy: assign the maximum possible digit (min(9, remaining)) at each position left to right.

This simultaneously:
1. Maximises sum of squares (concentrates digit sum into fewest, largest digits).
2. Produces the lexicographically largest number (since we use the largest valid digit first).

VALIDITY: s > 9*num is impossible (max digit sum = 9*num). s < 1 impossible (need ≥1 total).

COMPLEXITY: O(num) time, O(num) space for output string.

EDGE CASES:
  s = 9*num: all 9s.
  s = 1: "100...0" (1 followed by num-1 zeros).
  Single digit (num=1): valid iff 1 ≤ s ≤ 9.
