Title: Maximum Nesting Depth of Two Valid Parentheses Strings
URL: https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-nesting-depth-of-two-valid-parentheses-strings/

LLM Explanation:
APPROACH: Assign by depth parity — O(n) time, O(n) space.

KEY INSIGHT: To minimise max(depth(A), depth(B)), assign parentheses at even depths
to group 0 and odd depths to group 1 (or vice versa). This splits the nesting evenly.

For a '(' at depth d (after incrementing): assign to d % 2.
For a ')' at depth d (before decrementing): assign to d % 2.

CORRECTNESS: Each opening paren at depth d is matched with its closing paren at the
same depth. By alternating assignments, no group has consecutive nesting more than
half the original depth → max depth = ceil(original_depth / 2).

Both A and B remain valid VPS since each assigned '(' is paired with its corresponding
')' (same depth assignment for matching pairs).

COMPLEXITY: O(n) time, O(n) space for output.
