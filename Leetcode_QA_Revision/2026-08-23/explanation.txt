Title: Sum Game
URL: https://leetcode.com/problems/sum-game/

LLM Explanation:
APPROACH: Math / Greedy insight — reduce to a single closed-form condition.

KEY INSIGHT:
Both players play optimally. Consider what happens with the '?' characters:

1. If a '?' appears in the left half and one in the right half, Bob can always
   mirror Alice's move: whatever digit Alice places in one half, Bob places
   (9 - that digit) in the other, maintaining a net contribution of 9 to the
   difference left_sum - right_sum. These pairs cancel out strategically.

2. After pairing across halves, suppose there are excess '?'s all on one side.
   Alice tries to widen the gap; Bob tries to close it. For each pair of same-
   side '??', Bob can pick digits summing to 9 (e.g. 0+9 or 4+5) to add
   exactly 9 to that side's sum. Alice picks one digit, Bob picks the complement.

3. Bob wins (can always equalise) if and only if:
     left_sum + left_q * 4.5  ==  right_sum + right_q * 4.5
   i.e. left_sum - right_sum == (right_q - left_q) * 4.5
   Multiply by 2 to avoid fractions:
     2*(left_sum - right_sum) == (right_q - left_q) * 9

   This works because: pairs of '?' across halves cancel, and pairs within the
   same half each contribute 9 on average (Bob controls one of each pair).

Alice wins iff this equality does NOT hold.

CORRECTNESS VERIFICATION via parity: total '?' = left_q + right_q. The number
of cross-half pairs is min(left_q, right_q), leaving |left_q - right_q| same-
side '?', always even (since total '?' is even and paired off). Bob handles each
same-side pair optimally.

COMPLEXITY: O(n) time, O(1) extra space.
