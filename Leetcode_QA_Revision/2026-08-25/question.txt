Question:
Given an integer array nums and an integer k , return the smallest positive multiple of k that is missing from nums . A multiple of k is any positive integer divisible by k . Example 1: Input: nums = [8,2,3,4,6], k = 2 Output: 10 Explanation: The multiples of k = 2 are 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12... and the smallest multiple missing from nums is 10. Example 2: Input: nums = [1,4,7,10,15], k = 5 Output: 5 Explanation: The multiples of k = 5 are 5, 10, 15, 20... and the smallest multiple missing from nums is 5. Constraints: 1 &lt;= nums.length &lt;= 100 1 &lt;= nums[i] &lt;= 100 1 &lt;= k &lt;= 100
