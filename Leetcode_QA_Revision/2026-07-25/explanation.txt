Title: Maximum Product of Two Digits
URL: https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-product-of-two-digits/

LLM Explanation:
The problem asks for the maximum product of any two digits in a given number `n`. To maximize the product of two non-negative numbers, we should choose the two largest numbers available. In this case, the numbers are the digits of `n`.

The algorithm follows these steps:
1.  **Extract Digits**: The integer `n` is first converted into a string. This allows us to easily access each digit as a character.
2.  **Store Digits**: We iterate through the string of digits, convert each character back to an integer, and store them in a list.
3.  **Find Two Largest Digits**: To find the two largest digits efficiently, we sort the list of digits in ascending order. After sorting, the two largest digits will be the last two elements of the list.
4.  **Calculate Product**: Finally, we multiply these two largest digits to obtain the maximum possible product. The problem constraints (`10 <= n`) guarantee that there will always be at least two digits.

For example, if `n = 124`:
- The digits are `1`, `2`, and `4`.
- We create a list `[1, 2, 4]`.
- Sorting the list gives `[1, 2, 4]`.
- The two largest digits are the last two elements: `4` and `2`.
- The maximum product is `4 * 2 = 8`.

**Complexity Analysis:**
-   **Time Complexity**: `O(L log L)`, where `L` is the number of digits in `n`. This is dominated by the sorting step. Since `n <= 10^9`, `L` is at most 10, making the operation very fast and effectively constant time.
-   **Space Complexity**: `O(L)` to store the list of digits. This is also minimal given the constraints.
