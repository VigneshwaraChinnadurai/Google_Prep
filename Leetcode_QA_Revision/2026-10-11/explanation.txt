Title: Sum of Squares of Special Elements 
URL: https://leetcode.com/problems/sum-of-squares-of-special-elements/

LLM Explanation:
1-indexed: element at index i is special if n%i==0. Sum their squares. O(n) time, O(1) space.

LLM Testcase Validation:
Test 1: n=4. i=1✓,2✓,3✗,4✓ → 1²+2²+4²=1+4+16=21 ✓
Test 2: n=6. i=1✓,2✓,3✓,4✗,5✗,6✓ → 4+49+1+9=63 ✓
